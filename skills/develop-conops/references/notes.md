# Method rationale

Connect needs, boundaries, stakeholder responsibilities, scenarios, and evaluation plans. Scope integration to the engineer's current question. Scenario development remains independently invocable.

These instructions are an original project synthesis informed by systems-engineering study and the maintainer's design choices. General background includes Buede and Miller, *The Engineering Design of Systems: Models and Methods*. No textbook text, course reader, private annotation, or source-document attachment is distributed here. This is not a standardized or stakeholder-approved procedure.

The engineer directs consequential meanings, assumptions, priorities, and selections. Assistance can consist of targeted elicitation, investigation, a small model edit, or an explicit unknown. Prototypes should answer a specific uncertainty at the requested fidelity. Routine authorized edits and checks proceed without repeated confirmation.

Structured records, stable IDs, selected views, and program feedback are project implementation choices. They are not claims of measured token savings or universal computability. The bundled helper checks only its documented structure, completeness, and supported numeric conditions; it does not authenticate acceptance, prove real-world feasibility, or supply the planned formal verifier and protected runner.

Public background: [NASA requirements checklist](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/appendix-c-how-to-write-a-good-requirement/) and [NASA FRET](https://github.com/NASA-SW-VnV/fret). Those projects are independent; this package is not endorsed by NASA and currently contains no FRET implementation.
