# Joint-military symbology boundary

No official joint-military symbol assets are included in this candidate study. Its `field-*` drawings represent generic equipment and explanatory relationships; they do not encode affiliation, echelon, unit identity, operational status, or an authorized symbol identifier.

The [US Defense Logistics Agency record for MIL-STD-2525](https://quicksearch.dla.mil/qsDocDetails.aspx?ident_number=114934) identifies a separate joint-military symbology standard. A future standards-specific library needs a selected edition, the applicable semantic fields, a source and rights review, and item-by-item verification. The generic field drawings must not be represented as that library.
