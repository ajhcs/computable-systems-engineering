Computable Systems Engineering

Develop a connected model, inspect its engineering views, and check the work.

CSE connects eleven engineering workflows with deterministic checks for explicit models, calculations, queues, failures and measures. The engineer directs the meaning, scope and acceptance of the work.

Use with ChatGPT

The hosted MCP connection is https://cse.open-informatics.org/mcp. Custom connections depend on your ChatGPT account and workspace settings. A public Plugin Directory listing requires OpenAI review and publication.

Start with a question such as “Help me develop an operational scenario for a shared equipment service, including recovery from a missing return.” Provide selected non-restricted source material and keep assumptions visible.

The service processes supplied text in temporary jobs. It does not retain projects or read files from your computer. Check results cover the declared representation and supplied evidence; they do not establish physical validity, regulatory compliance or engineering acceptance.
