Privacy

Effective November 15, 2026.

This policy covers the hosted Computable Systems Engineering service at cse.open-informatics.org, maintained by the ajhcs CSE project.

Data and purpose

The service receives the model text, record contents, selected filenames, identifiers and analysis parameters submitted in each tool request. It uses them to run the requested engineering check or generate the requested view. No account, personal profile, cookies, advertising identifiers or payment details are requested. Do not submit PHI, credentials, payment-card data, government identifiers or other restricted data.

Processing and recipients

Requests pass through ChatGPT and Cloudflare to the engineering runtime. Cloudflare processes connection metadata, including IP addresses, to deliver, secure and rate-limit the service. Application access logging and container logs are disabled, and the application does not record request bodies or tool results. The runtime cannot fetch arbitrary external URLs. The project does not sell request data, build advertising profiles or use submissions to train models.

Retention

The application has no project database. Request files and generated artifacts are deleted when the job finishes, including on a handled failure or timeout. Jobs have a 30-second execution limit. If the process crashes before cleanup, temporary files can remain until the next job clears the request directory or the container filesystem is discarded. No persistent project volume, snapshot or application recovery archive is created. Results returned to ChatGPT remain subject to your ChatGPT conversation retention and settings.

Cloudflare and OpenAI have separate infrastructure and platform practices, including operational data retention. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy and OpenAI’s privacy policy. This application does not control those providers’ retention settings.

Your controls

You choose the material sent with each call and can disconnect the plugin. Use the local edition when material must stay on your computer. Keep generated files in storage you control and manage conversations through ChatGPT. For privacy questions, open a minimal support issue without including private models or personal data. GitHub retains submitted issue content under its own policies until it is deleted or otherwise removed.
