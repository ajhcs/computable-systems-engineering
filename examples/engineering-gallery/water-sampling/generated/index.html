Read the operation

The operator presents a labeled container. The station records its identity and returns a receipt. The courier reconciles the pickup manifest and obtains a custody record. An unreadable label instead causes a hold; corrected identity precedes a new receipt. A custodian suspends intake for inspection; release back to operation remains a separate decision.

Functions, not execution order

One root, four branches and two subordinate functions per branch. Parentage and allocation are documentary; components and functions retain distinct types. Open the full-width view and scroll rather than shrink labels.

What was checked
