# Matched diagram presentation derivatives

Five presentation directions apply to four selected lifecycle, timing, composition
and optical views: Technical Ledger, Instrument Record, Quiet Precision, Research
Folio and Engineering Standard. These provide twenty matched presentations, plus
four Instrument Record light-print views and two Action controls (Technical Ledger
and Engineering Standard). The two earlier Sequence controls remain local with the
baseline Sequence picture pending corrected supported rendering.

Existing diagrams preserve selected source IDs, semantic text, containment,
endpoints, glyphs, cardinalities and coordinates. They are simplified v1-style
model-derived presentations; separately authored v2 models and actual pilot views
have distinct labels. [Base readings](../engineering-reference/README.md),
[Action/Sequence scope](../engineering-reference-extension/README.md) and
[source notices](../engineering-reference/notices/README.md) accompany them.
No private style specimens, brand assets, original source figures or raw model
records are bundled. Palette names do not imply endorsement or a winning style.

Each treatment has SVG, PNG, draw.io and matching geometry. Open [review.html](review.html)
for available baseline/treatment switches and text alternatives. Formal diagrams
retain intrinsic width and horizontal scrolling. Existing A3, color-vision,
viewport and grayscale experiments remain historical local evidence; no fresh
browser run, human comprehension result or readable A4/Letter claim is made here.

`python3 tests/test_diagram_style_prototypes.py` checks selected non-color
semantics/geometry and meaningful negative mutations. Regeneration with
`python3 scripts/render_diagram_style_prototypes.py` emits candidates only; any
changed SVG/PNG/editable pair requires supported rendering and inspection before
release. Raw-original-source comparison is separate from these derivative checks.
