# A first concept for a community tool library

Help me develop the initial system concept for our small community tool library. Focus this session on essential functions and comparing the two candidate approaches below. I want to decide which direction to explore after seeing the comparison.

Members frequently arrive to find that a tool marked available has already been borrowed. Volunteers record loans and returns in a paper ledger, but the separate availability board is updated irregularly. We have about 40 members, usually one volunteer on duty, and a two-hour Saturday opening window. A volunteer estimated that each loan or return takes three minutes. They also estimated ten arrivals in the busiest half-hour; these are recollections, not measurements.

We can spend at most $200 upfront and have no recurring software budget. An old laptop is available; its condition and maintenance burden have not been assessed. Internet access sometimes fails. Members should be able to check availability, but other borrowers' identities should not be publicly displayed. Our existing rule is that the on-duty volunteer authorizes each loan; preserve that rule.

Compare an improved paper ledger and availability-board workflow with a local offline ledger on the existing laptop. I have not agreed on a maximum acceptable wait or weights for cost, convenience, volunteer workload, or reliability. Our desired outcome is fewer wasted member visits and less volunteer administration.

Keep the concept brief and assumptions visible. Detailed operational scenarios and a complete CONOPS are outside this session's scope. Use a structured record if the skill calls for one, and show me the consequential decision I need to make next.
