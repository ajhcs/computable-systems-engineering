Synthetic shared-tool lending service, proposed solely for a CSE method test.
Boundary: request assessment, checkout recording, fault withdrawal, maintenance
coordination and return-to-service evaluation. External participants are a
borrower and a maintenance provider. Procurement, disposal, inventory sizing,
staffing adequacy, safety certification and performance thresholds are deferred.
Need: access to tools, including explicit handling of reported tool faults.
Normal situation: borrower requests a tool; desk operator assesses availability;
checkout application records the handover and return instructions.
Fault situation: borrower reports a fault; desk operator records the report and
withdraws the tool. No repair time or automatic return to service is promised.
Lifecycle situation: maintenance provider follows the maintenance procedure at
the service bench; a separate readiness evaluation is planned before release.
Means: desk operator, checkout application, tool store, maintenance provider,
maintenance procedure, service bench and readiness test kit. These are distinct
responsibilities/resources, not an assertion that seven people are required.
Interface: checkout application exchanges availability/fault records with the
tool-store operation; maintenance provider receives a fault/work record.
Evaluation: observe an ordinary checkout and a reported-fault walkthrough;
rehearse maintenance and readiness evaluation in a nonoperational test setting.
Required preparations include a desk operator, a service bench, a maintenance
procedure, a maintenance provider and a readiness test kit. All evaluation
records are plans with results not_run. Test criteria and actual capability
remain unresolved; passing the documentary review cannot authorize operation.
