# Attribution, modifications and source scope

The C/D/E models, selected fact ledgers, presentation notes, aliases and custom layouts are adaptations produced and revised in October 2026. Release packaging, provenance and supporting tools were modified on 2026-10-27. Retained models, recorded linked facts, SVG/PNG and draw.io/view geometry keep their historical bytes; none is an original author-created SysML v2 model, a native-tool export, or an endorsement. Raw source XML, archives, publications and original author figures are not included.

**C — NASA.** Credit NASA, *NASA Systems Engineering Handbook*, NASA SP-6105, June 1995, Figure B-10. The [official NASA record](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19960002194) marks this work “Work of the US Gov. Public Use Permitted.” The selected figure has no identified third-party credit exception. Follow [NASA's media policy](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/) for attribution and nonendorsement. The maintenance model and layout are explanatory adaptations, not NASA-approved procedures.

[Publication](https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=904712#page=33); [NIST technical-series reuse policy](https://www.nist.gov/open/copyright-fair-use-and-licensing-statements-srd-data-software-and-technical-series-publications).
